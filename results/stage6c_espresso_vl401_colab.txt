##### LAB 4  MULTI-LEVEL OPTIMIZATION (SIS)  (event_priority_min.sop = espresso result) #####
 STEP 1  literals as sum of products = 34,  as best factored form = 18
 STEP 2  Factored form of each output
     VS = r v
     VP = m p u (v' + r')
     AS = a b' p' (v' + r')
     AP = e p' (v' + r') (b + a')
 STEP 3  Kernels: VP kernel r'+v' (co-kernel mpu); AS kernel r'+v' (co-kernel ab'p');
          AP kernels b+a' (co-kernels ep'v', ep'r'), r'+v' (a'ep', bep'),
          br'+bv'+a'r'+a'v' (ep', level 1)
 STEP 4  Kernel extraction (gkx)
     VS = r v      VP = t4 m p u      AS = t4 a b' p'      AP = t4 t5 e p'
     t4 = r' + v'  t5 = a' + b
 STEP 5  Cube extraction (gcx)
     t4 = e p'   t5 = a b' p'   t6 = m p u
 STEP 6  Full SIS optimization script (script.rugged)
     VS = r v
     VP = VS' m p u
     AS = VS' a b' p'
     AP = VS' AS' e p'
 STEP 7  Summary                   literals (sop)
  input (SOP)                      34
  after kernel extraction (gkx)    18
  after cube extraction (gcx)      30
  after script.rugged              14

Interpretation: espresso's two-level cover (9 AND gates, 34 literals) is the
exact minimum. SIS script.rugged rebuilds the priority chain VS > VP/AS > AP
(VS' gates the lower-priority outputs, AS' gates AP) in 14 literals -- the
same structure the pacemaker specification states. Note VP = VS' m p u needs
no VP' term in AS/AP because VP requires p while AS/AP require p'.
